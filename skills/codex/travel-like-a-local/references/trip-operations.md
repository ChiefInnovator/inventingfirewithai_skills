# Booking, dining, and transport details

Use the sections relevant to the current request.

- **Make costs transparent.** For trip proposals, itemize lodging by property, room type, and night; transfers by route; activities; and agency or service fees. Show taxes, inclusions, exclusions, cancellation terms, currency, and total. Compare equivalent direct-booking options where useful. Flag unexplained markups and distinguish estimates from quotes. Willingness to pay for quality does not imply willingness to accept opaque bundles, unlimited spending, or a permanent refusal to pay a clearly justified service fee.
- **Use transfers thoughtfully.** A scenic lunch or tasting stop can enrich an already necessary transfer when it fits the route and the traveler’s energy. Show the added time, detour, cost, luggage arrangements, and reservation needs. Do not turn an efficient transfer into an exhausting excursion or assume a past interest in a countryside drive overrides the traveler’s current wish to relax.
- **Search booking records thoroughly.** Search all accessible relevant mail folders, including travel folders, sent messages, and archives, rather than only the inbox. Follow related correspondence and amendments. Disclose connector or folder coverage limits; a missing search result is not proof that a booking or reply does not exist.
- **Maintain one reconciled itinerary.** When asked for the traveler’s plans, review the latest relevant messages and the traveler’s corrections. Track each item's local date/time, location, status, source, and outstanding action. Resolve amendments, cancellations, and rebookings in sequence instead of listing each email as a separate activity. Flag conflicting records precisely. A confirmation the user gives remains user-confirmed even if you cannot locate its email; say “not independently verified in the records reviewed,” not “unconfirmed.”
- **Separate communication from commitment.** Keep drafts, sent inquiries, supplier replies, availability, holds, and confirmed bookings distinct. Interest in a car, tour, or treatment is not a booking. An unanswered concierge question is still open; a draft reply has not reached the recipient. Summarize what changed and what still needs a response.
- **Compare the actual meal.** When choosing among restaurants, show current menus or useful translations, service format, representative dishes, prices, duration, and meaningful differences. When the user supplies a menu, recommend a specific order that fits the traveler’s stated tastes and occasion. Verify which dining room and menu a reservation covers. Account for substantial afternoon tea or a long tasting menu when scheduling other meals.
- **Check every option against the actual day.** Apply the current-operation and temporary-closure checks in [venue-research.md](venue-research.md), including Google Maps and Yelp where available, to alternatives as well as the first choice. A restaurant open tomorrow may be unusable tonight. For a short stay, allocate limited opportunities around operating days, existing reservations, and desired variety. Offer a feasible fallback without implying it is reserved.
- **Make hotel logistics usable.** When relevant, verify breakfast location, hours, format, and inclusion; checkout and agreed exceptions; luggage arrangements; pickup location and time; journey duration; and the next property's arrival requirements. Distinguish checkout time from vehicle pickup. Identify gaps needing a luggage or waiting plan rather than assuming they are errors. Compare an airport overnight with staying centrally only when flight timing makes that decision useful.
- **Treat remote transport as a real constraint.** Do not assume ride-hailing, taxis, or a convenient return ride will be available. Before recommending an off-property outing, verify realistic transport options, advance-booking needs, round-trip cost and duration, and the return arrangement. Compare the outing with an appealing on-property alternative. If transport cannot be verified, label that uncertainty and keep the on-property plan as the default. Avoid stacking distant activities or using all free time for excursions during a restorative stay.
- **Use destination time consistently.** Resolve “today,” “tonight,” and weekdays from the traveler’s current location. Use its local time throughout the itinerary. Keep any response timestamp in the user-requested time zone, clearly labeled, accounting for whether the request specifies a fixed offset or daylight-saving local time.

- **Honor authorized reservation terms.** When the user has authorized a booking and acceptance of standard terms, carry that authorization through the booking flow without asking again. Check the actual time, party size, seating, menu, price, cancellation/no-show terms, and confirmation result. Do not add extras or optional marketing consent. Surface a new material charge or commitment outside the authorized scope before accepting it. Submit dietary notes in the requested language and remove restrictions the user has withdrawn.
- **Answer pickup-time questions from the latest confirmation.** Search the supplier thread and amendments before answering. Lead with the confirmed local date, pickup time and location, destination terminal, and price if relevant. Distinguish the booked pickup from a newly calculated recommended departure time. Keep separate travelers' transfers separate; do not substitute a companion's departure for the user's.
- **Calculate airport timing from the actual itinerary.** Verify the operating carrier, first flight's local departure date/time, departure terminal, check-in and bag-drop opening times and deadlines, baggage, and realistic transfer duration. Work backward from a suitable airport-arrival buffer. Business class does not establish that a counter or lounge will be open. Verify lounge access, hours, and airside zone for the actual departure gate; do not assume a lounge in another zone is usable.

- **Match nutritional goals only when supplied.** For a protein-focused request, compare substantial suitable mains and available portions against the traveler's stated target. Label nutritional estimates and distinguish food weight from protein grams. Do not prescribe a universal diet or carry withdrawn restrictions forward. Apply cuisine, seating, and preparation constraints from the current request or private profile.
